# Vendored: `hidpp`

This crate is a vendored copy of the `hidpp` crate from
<https://github.com/lus/logy> (commit `135c5600807845c269b5d5bfa1f33032281fbd86`,
2025-12-26), written by Lukas Schulte Pelkum and released under the
BSD Zero Clause License (see `LICENSE` in this directory).

BoltKit carries local modifications on top of the upstream code: the `0x1b04`
reprogrammable-controls feature, Bolt pairing status and long codenames,
dependency updates and fixes to the channel's read loop and software IDs.
They are licensed under the same 0BSD terms as the original.
